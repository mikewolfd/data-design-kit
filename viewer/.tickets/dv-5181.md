# Lead dataset pages with purpose, record meaning, limits and next action

Requirements: PCR-05 — product requirements (`outputs/public-catalog-research-20261107/product-requirements.md`).

Owner role: Viewer overview and publisher copy. Delivery phase: phase-1.

Current baseline: The header and Overview already contain summaries, row meaning, coverage, access and technical details. This task improves their order and removes repetition.

Implementation scope:
- Make the opening content answer the four visitor questions before the detailed schema.
- Separate purpose from the record unit; retain supplied measure units and denominators where they distinguish datasets.
- Keep consequential limits visible, define unfamiliar terms, and keep source descriptions and technical identifiers expandable.

Example: “Access to electricity (% of population)” identifies a measure and denominator in its title. **Proposed copy:** “Witness appearances” followed by “A reported appearance at a meeting or in a document. The same person may appear more than once,” subject to the publisher's exact occurrence definition. [World Bank indicator](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS)

## Acceptance Criteria

- A visitor can identify the record unit and supported next action from the first content section.
- Summary and row meaning are not duplicates; unavailable facts display precise missing states.
- Synthetic/example labels remain visible, and an unavailable record path does not prompt an impossible version choice.
- Mobile and keyboard order match the intended reading order; exact source text remains accessible.
